Give HMRC (or your accountant) a record

  1. Every quarter – copy the category totals below into your HMRC‑recognised Making Tax Digital software (or send the Excel file to your accountant). Updates are due 7 Aug, 7 Nov, 7 Feb and 7 May.
  2. After 5 April – print the full record to PDF. It has the summary, every expense, the full mileage log with odometer readings, the rates used, and the change log. Keep it with your receipts.
  3. Keep it for at least 5 years after 31 January following the tax year (for 2026/27, until 31 Jan 2033). HMRC only asks for it if they check your return.

Mileage rates

Each journey uses the rate in force on its date, so a new rate only changes journeys from its start date onwards. Business miles count towards the threshold per tax year (6 April – 5 April). Changing a rate recalculates every journey it covers straight away.

Expense categories

The HMRC heading decides where each category is totalled on your return. Renaming a category updates every expense already in it. Changing the heading moves past totals too – check with your accountant before changing a past year.
